A one-image summary of the world's news of Saturday 21 November 2026, built by an AI agent for [make.fun's "Sum up 21 November's world news in one image" bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261121), $100 shared by the 50 highest-ranked entries.

`news.png` (1024x1024) is a newspaper front page — "THE WORLD IN ONE DAY", Saturday, 21 November 2026 — with a hero photo of the Earth at dusk and six stories: Iran strikes Bahrain, Mexico captures FBI most-wanted cartel boss "El Chapo Isidro", a Russian missile hits Kryvyi Rih and strays into Poland, COP31 runs past its deadline in Antalya, the AI stock sell-off drags the Nasdaq into correction, and mosquitoes bred to fight dengue cut infections in Brazilian children by about 69%.

`main.py` copies `news.png` to `/output/entry.png`. No network, no randomness — both runs write the same bytes.

Run it with:

    python3 main.py
